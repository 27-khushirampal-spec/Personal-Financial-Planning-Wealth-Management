# Personal Financial Planning & Wealth Management

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive individual financial planning case study for a hypothetical client, Mr. Sanjay Sharma, a 35-year-old professional based in Gurugram, Haryana.

The study evaluates the client's financial position, family responsibilities, income, expenditure, assets, liabilities and risk profile to develop a structured financial plan aligned with short-, medium- and long-term goals.

## Objectives

- Assess the client's current financial position through cash flow and net worth analysis.
- Identify and prioritise short-, medium- and long-term financial goals.
- Evaluate risk tolerance, liquidity requirements and insurance coverage.
- Develop goal-based investment strategies and monthly SIP requirements.
- Recommend asset allocation and strategies for long-term wealth creation and financial security.

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Financial Profiling
Assessment of the client's life-cycle stage, income stability, family responsibilities, savings capacity, existing liabilities and financial priorities.

### 2. Cash Flow and Net Worth Analysis
Preparation of income and expenditure statements, an annual cash budget and a balance sheet to evaluate savings capacity and financial strength.

### 3. Risk Profiling and Asset Allocation
Assessment of the client's risk profile as moderately aggressive, with a proposed broad asset allocation of:
- Equity: 55%
- Debt / Fixed Income: 30%
- Liquid / Cash: 15%

### 4. Insurance and Risk Management
Evaluation of emergency reserves, family health insurance and life insurance requirements, including an assessment using the Human Life Value (HLV) method.

### 5. Goal-Based Financial Planning
Identification of financial goals related to:
- Emergency fund and insurance protection
- Children's higher education and marriage
- Retirement planning
- Medical reserves for parents
- Home and vehicle-related commitments
- Wealth creation and financial independence

### 6. Investment Planning
Estimation of monthly SIP requirements for future goals using assumed return rates and future value calculations, alongside a proposed monthly surplus allocation.

## Key Findings

- The household has a positive monthly surplus of ₹55,000 and a reported net worth of ₹1.367 crore.
- A substantial portion of the family's wealth is concentrated in its residential property.
- Insurance adequacy, future education expenses, retirement needs and outstanding loans require coordinated planning.
- Goal-based investing can help allocate the available surplus across competing financial priorities.
- An appropriate mix of equity, fixed income and liquid assets is proposed based on the client's circumstances and risk profile.
